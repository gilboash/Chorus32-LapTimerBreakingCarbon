What is new in 3.0.0

The buzzer and receiver multiplexing both default to off. Multiplexing time-shared one receiver across several pilots and credited mistimed samples to the wrong one, which showed up as a pilot scoring another's laps. With it off, a pilot enabled beyond the receiver count is not timed, and the race page now warns when that happens. The default race length is 20 laps and timing continues past the flag.
